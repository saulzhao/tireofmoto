About ZENYARE

Professional motorcycle tire manufacturing meets practical rider education.

Company Profile

ZENYARE is a professional manufacturer of motorcycle tires and inner tubes, operated by QINGDAO ZHENGYA RUBBER INDUSTRIAL CO., LTD.

This website (Tire of Moto) shares practical knowledge about tire sizing, tube valves, fitment tips, and sourcing guidance for distributors and motorcycle users worldwide.

What We Cover

  • Tire size explanation (metric & inch sizes)
  • Tube selection checklist (size / valve / angle)
  • Installation & maintenance tips
  • Buying and sourcing guidance for wholesale customers
  • Product comparisons and recommendations

Our Mission

We believe every rider deserves accurate, practical information about motorcycle tires. Whether you're a commuter replacing a punctured tube or a distributor sourcing products for your market, we aim to be your trusted reference.

Main Products

  • 🔧 Inner Tubes (Butyl / Natural)
  • ⚙️ Valve Types: TR4 / TR13 / AV
