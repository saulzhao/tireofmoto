Choosing a Wider Tire

This tool only converts units. It can't tell you whether a size fits your motorcycle.

  • Start from the size in your owner's manual or the tire maker's fitment list. Dunlop says fitting wider tires is "possible in some cases, but again, it should only be done with the approval of the motorcycle or tire manufacturer."
  • Check the rim: tire makers list the rim widths approved for each tire model and size.
  • Check clearance: Dunlop says you must factor in "clearance for width and diameter, the effect on stability and handling, along with whether your rim is wide enough."
