How to Convert

Inches = Millimeters / 25.4

Example: 120mm / 25.4 = 4.72 inches

Fitting Wider Tires

  • Front: +/- 5mm of OEM is generally safe
  • Rear: +/- 10mm (wider = more grip, slower steering)
  • Always check clearance with forks and swingarm
