Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Who runs this site

Tire of Moto is run by ZENYARE, a manufacturer of motorcycle tires and inner tubes (see our About page). This policy explains what information the site handles.

What we collect

You can read the site and use the tire calculators without an account, and the calculators run in your browser. We don't use an analytics service, and the site does not set cookies or use browser storage.

Contact form and email

The contact form does not send anything to our server. When you press send, it opens your own email app with your message filled in, addressed to zenyaretyre@gmail.com. If you send it, we receive your name, email address, any WhatsApp number you add and your message. We use them only to reply to your enquiry and keep them in our mailbox for as long as needed to handle it.

Hosting and technical logs

Like any website, Tire of Moto is delivered through a hosting and network provider that processes technical information such as IP addresses, browser type and request details in order to deliver and protect the site.

Advertising and affiliate links

The site does not currently show ads or contain affiliate links. If we add advertising such as Google AdSense, we will update this policy before the ads go live, explaining how third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites, and how you can opt out of personalized advertising. Any affiliate links would be disclosed as described on our disclosure page.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here and update the date at the top. For privacy questions, email zenyaretyre@gmail.com.
