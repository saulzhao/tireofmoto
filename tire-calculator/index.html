How to Read Your Tire Size

Look at the sidewall of your tire. You'll see something like 90/90-18 or 3.00-18. The numbers before the dash are the width measurements.

Valve Type Guide

  • TR4: Straight valve stem - most common on motorcycles
  • TR13: Angled 90° valve - used when disc brakes block straight access
  • AV/Schrader: Same as car tire valve - common on larger motorcycles
