Tube Buying Checklist: Size, Valve & Angle

January 18, 2026 · Read article
𝕏 Share Facebook Copy Link

Buying the wrong inner tube is frustrating and wastes money. Follow this simple 5-step checklist to get it right every time.

Step 1: Check Your Current Tire Size

Look at the sidewall of your current tire. You’ll see something like 90/90-18 or 3.00-18. Write this down — the new tube must match.

Step 2: Identify Valve Type

Remove the valve cap and look at the valve stem:

  • Straight stem: Likely TR4 (most common) or AV/Schrader
  • Angled 90°: TR13 (used when disc brakes block straight access)
  • Thin metal stem with nut: Presta (rare on motorcycles)

Step 3: Measure Stem Length

Most motorcycle tubes use 32mm stems. Some deep-dish rims need 48mm stems. Measure from the rim surface to the valve tip.

Step 4: Choose Rubber Compound

MaterialProsPrice Range
ButylBest air retention, durable$5 – $12
Natural RubberFlexible, budget-friendly$3 – $8
LatexUltra-low pressure loss, rare$15 – $25

Step 5: Quantity & Brand

Buy in multiples. Keep at least 2 spare tubes on hand for touring. Stick with established brands for consistent quality.

Complete Checklist Summary

✅ Final Purchase Checklist

  • Tire size matches (e.g., 90/90-18)
  • Valve type matches (TR4 straight or TR13 angled)
  • Stem length correct (32mm or 48mm)
  • Butyl rubber preferred for best air retention
  • Buy from a reputable brand
  • Get at least 2 tubes for touring
  • Include a valve core removal tool
