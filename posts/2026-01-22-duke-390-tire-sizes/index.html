Introduction

If you're looking for tire size information for the KTM Duke 390 Tire Sizes: Front & Rear Guide (All Years), you've come to the right place. Whether you're doing a scheduled replacement or upgrading your ride, knowing exactly which tire sizes fit your motorcycle is critical for safety and performance.

Correct Tire Sizes for Your Motorcycle

The manufacturer specifies exact front and rear tire sizes for each model year. Here are the standard specifications:

  • Front tire size: Standard sportbike sizing with optimized contact patch width
  • Rear tire size: Wide profile for maximum grip during acceleration and cornering
  • Load index: Meets or exceeds the original equipment specification for your bike's weight plus cargo
  • Speed rating: Sufficient for legal road speeds plus safety margin

Why Using the Correct Tire Size Matters

Using the wrong tire size on your motorcycle can cause several serious problems: incorrect speedometer reading, altered steering geometry, reduced ground clearance, brake interference, and potential suspension damage. Always stick to manufacturer-specified sizes or consult a tire professional for approved alternative sizes.

Best Tire Options for This Model

When shopping for replacement tires, consider these top picks based on riding style:

  1. Daily commuting / street riding: Look for tires with wet weather grip ratings and long tread life compounds
  2. Sport riding / track days: Choose softer compounds with aggressive tread patterns for maximum cornering grip
  3. Touring / long-distance: Prioritize high-mileage ratings, hydroplaning resistance and comfortable ride quality
  4. Dual-sport / adventure: Select tires with mixed street/off-road tread that handles gravel and light trails

Tire Replacement Tips

When replacing motorcycle tires, always check the DOT manufacture date (the last 4 digits on the sidewall). Tires older than 5 years should be replaced regardless of tread depth. Have your bike's tire mounted and balanced by a professional to ensure proper seating and safe operation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use a different tire size than stock?

Minor deviations are sometimes possible but can affect handling, ABS calibration and speedometer accuracy. Consult the tire manufacturer's fitment guide before deviating from OEM sizes.

How often should motorcycle tires be replaced?

Replace motorcycle tires every 3-5 years by age or when tread depth reaches the wear indicator bars, whichever comes first. Typical mileage ranges from 3,000 miles for sport compounds to 20,000+ miles for touring compounds.

What tire pressure should I set?

Check your motorcycle's owner manual or the sticker on the swingarm/fairing for factory-recommended PSI. Generally front tires run lower than rear tires. Always check pressure when tires are cold.
