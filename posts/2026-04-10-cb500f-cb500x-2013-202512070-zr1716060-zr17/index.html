Honda CB500F and CB500X Tire Sizes by Year

April 10, 2026 · By Zenyare Tire
𝕏 Share Facebook Copy Link

The Honda CB500F and CB500X share a rear tire size, 160/60-17, but not always a front. In every Honda spec we checked, the CB500F uses a 120/70-17 front. In the 2015, 2017 and 2018 CB500X specs we checked, the front is 120/70-17; for the 2019 model year Honda fitted a 19-inch front wheel with a 110/80-19 front tire.

Below are the sizes by model and year, as Honda printed them. We only list years where we found an official Honda specification.

Honda Europe's specifications note that "All specifications are provisional and subject to change without notice."

What changed on the 2019 CB500X?

The front wheel. Honda Europe's 2019 press release says: "The 17-inch front wheel has been replaced with a 19-inch," and that "the lightweight aluminium rim wears a 110/80-R19 trail-pattern tyre, the 17-inch rear a 160/60-R17 tyre." American Honda's 2019 announcement says the same in fewer words: "The front wheel is now 19 inches."

A 120/70-17 front tire is made for a 17-inch rim, so it isn't the size for a 2019-or-later CB500X front wheel.

Which models does Honda UK sell now?

Honda UK's current range doesn't list models called CB500F or CB500X. It lists a CB500 Hornet and an NX500, with these sizes:

If you own one of these, use the sizes for your model name from Honda's page or your manual.

What about tire pressure?

None of the Honda specifications we checked lists tire pressures. Use the figures in your owner's manual or on the tire information label on your bike.

How to read the sizes

  • 120/70-17: 120 mm nominal width, sidewall height 70% of the width, 17-inch rim.
  • 110/80-19: 110 mm nominal width, sidewall height 80% of the width, 19-inch rim.
  • 160/60-17: 160 mm nominal width, sidewall height 60% of the width, 17-inch rim.
  • Markings such as (58W) or "On-off pattern": as printed by Honda. Follow your owner's manual for the markings to match.

Can I fit a different size?

Only use a size approved by the motorcycle manufacturer or tire maker. Dunlop says a change should only be made "with the approval of the motorcycle or tire manufacturer."

Buying checklist for a CB500F or CB500X

  1. Confirm your model and model year from your registration or manual.
  2. CB500X: check the front wheel. If it's a 19-inch wheel, you need a 19-inch front tire.
  3. Check the size on your current tires' sidewalls and compare it with the table for your year.
  4. If your year isn't listed above, confirm the sizes in your owner's manual.
  5. Set cold pressures from your manual or the label on the bike.

FAQ

Do the CB500F and CB500X use the same tires?

The rear size is the same, 160/60-17. The front matches in the 2015, 2017 and 2018 CB500X specs we checked. From the 2019 model year, Honda lists a 110/80-19 front for the CB500X.

What front tire does a 2019 or later CB500X take?

Honda lists 110/80-19 (Honda Europe: 110/80R19M/C) on a 19 X MT2.5 rim.

What tire size does the Honda CB500F take?

Honda lists 120/70-17 front and 160/60-17 rear in every CB500F specification we checked, from 2013 to 2024.
