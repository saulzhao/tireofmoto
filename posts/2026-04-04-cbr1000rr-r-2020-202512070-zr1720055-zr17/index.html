Honda CBR1000RR-R Fireblade (2020) and Fireblade SP (2024) Tire Sizes

Published on April 4, 2026

The CBR1000RR-R Fireblade arrived for 2020 as a new model, not an update of the old CBR1000RR. One of the changes Honda pointed out was the rear tire: it went up to a 200/55-ZR17. This page covers the two versions for which we found Honda's own published specification, the 2020 Fireblade and the 2024 Fireblade SP, and what you still need to check on your own bike.

We only list a figure when it appears in an official Honda document. Anything we could not source is left out.

Both Honda documents give the same tire and rim sizes. The SP is the higher-spec version, with Öhlins S-EC3.0 suspension on the 2024 model according to Honda UK, but its tire and rim sizes in Honda's 2024 listing match the 2020 standard model.

Original-equipment tires listed by Honda

  • 2020 Fireblade press release: Pirelli Diablo Supercorsa SP or Bridgestone RS11, front and rear.
  • 2024 Fireblade SP (Honda UK): Pirelli Diablo Supercorsa SP (V3) or Bridgestone RS11, front and rear.

The fitted brand can vary by market. These are the tires Honda lists, not a recommendation from us.

What changed in 2020

Honda's 2020 press release says the rear 17-inch rim "mounts a 200/55-ZR17 sized tyre (from 190/50-ZR17)". In other words, the previous CBR1000RR Fireblade used a 190/50-ZR17 rear. Honda explains the change as "minimising the change in chassis geometry when going from street to track rubber."

The front stayed at 120/70-ZR17.

So if you are moving from an older Fireblade to a CBR1000RR-R, do not assume the old rear tire will fit. The rear size is different.

ABS on these two models

  • 2020 Fireblade: the press release lists "2 Channel" ABS and says the system gains "two switchable modes", SPORT and TRACK.
  • 2024 Fireblade SP: Honda UK lists the ABS type as "2 channel with IMU".

If your bike is a different year or version

We did not find an official Honda specification page or owner's manual for the 2020 SP, the 2021–2023 models, the 2024 standard Fireblade or the 2025 models that we could read and cite. Their tire sizes may well match, but we are not going to state that without Honda's own text.

For any other year or version, read the tire information label on the bike or the specifications section of your owner's manual. Those are written for your exact model and market.

Tire pressure, load index and speed rating

Neither Honda document lists tire pressures or load indexes. Use the pressures on the label on your bike or in your owner's manual. When buying tires, match the load index and speed symbol given there and never go below them. Do not take a pressure from a forum or a different model.

Can I fit a different size?

Only use a size approved by the motorcycle manufacturer or tire maker. Dunlop says a change should only be made "with the approval of the motorcycle or tire manufacturer."

This includes fitting the old 190/50-ZR17 rear from the previous Fireblade. Honda changed the rear size on purpose, so any change back needs approval from Honda or the tire maker.

Buying checklist for a CBR1000RR-R

  1. Read the size, load index and speed symbol from your owner's manual or the tire label on the bike.
  2. Confirm whether you have the standard Fireblade or the SP, and which model year and market.
  3. For the 2020 Fireblade and 2024 SP, Honda lists a 200/55-ZR17 rear. Don't fit anything else unless Honda or the tire maker has approved it.
  4. Check the manufacture date on any tire you buy (the last four digits of the DOT code) and follow the tire maker's guidance on age.
  5. Replace the tire when the tread reaches the wear indicator bars, or earlier if it is damaged.

FAQ

What tire size does the Honda CBR1000RR-R Fireblade use?

Honda lists 120/70-ZR17 at the front and 200/55-ZR17 at the rear for the 2020 Fireblade and the 2024 Fireblade SP.

What rim width does the CBR1000RR-R have?

For those two models, Honda lists 17 x 3.5 inches at the front and 17 x 6.0 inches at the rear.

Is the CBR1000RR-R rear tire the same as the old Fireblade?

No. Honda says the CBR1000RR-R moved to a 200/55-ZR17 rear, from 190/50-ZR17 on the previous model.

Can I use different tire sizes?

Only use a size approved by the motorcycle manufacturer or tire maker. Dunlop says a change should only be made "with the approval of the motorcycle or tire manufacturer."

What tire pressure should I run?

Use the figures on the tire information label or in your owner's manual. Honda's press release and spec page do not list pressures.
