BMW R 1250 GS and R 1300 GS Tire Sizes: What BMW's Rider's Manuals Say

March 23, 2026·Read article
❉ ShareFacebookCopy Link

BMW's rider's manuals for the R 1250 GS, the R 1250 GS Adventure, the R 1300 GS and the R 1300 GS Adventure all list the same tire sizes: 120/70 R 19 at the front on a 3.00" x 19" rim, and 170/60 R 17 at the rear on a 4.50" x 17" rim. The listed cold pressures are 2.5 bar (36.3 psi) front and 2.9 bar (42.1 psi) rear. What differs between models is the wheel type, not the size.

Every figure below comes from BMW Motorrad's own rider's manuals, which BMW publishes as PDFs. We list the editions we read at the end.

Where the manuals mention load, they give the same pressures for riding solo and two-up. Your own manual and the label on the bike come first; check them if your bike differs from what's shown here.

How to read the sizes

  • 120/70 R 19: 120 mm nominal width, sidewall height 70% of the width, R for radial, 19-inch rim.
  • 170/60 R 17: 170 mm nominal width, sidewall height 60% of the width, R for radial, 17-inch rim.

A replacement tire needs at least the load index (60 front, 72 rear) and speed category (V) listed in the manual.

Which wheels does each model use?

The sizes are the same; the wheel types listed in the manuals differ by model and option:

ModelWheel types listed in BMW's manuals
R 1250 GSAluminium cast wheels; cross-spoked wheels as an option
R 1250 GS AdventureCross-spoked wheels
R 1300 GSAluminium cast wheels; cross-spoked wheels or forged aluminium enduro wheels as options
R 1300 GS AdventureCross-spoked wheels; forged aluminium enduro wheels as an option

Every wheel type has the same rim and tire sizes in the manuals. If you have spoked wheels, check your manual or the wheel itself for whether your rims take tubeless tires before you buy.

R 1250 GS vs R 1300 GS: did the tire size change?

No. BMW's R 1300 GS manuals list the same front and rear tire sizes, rim sizes, load indexes, speed category and cold pressures as the R 1250 GS manuals. One small difference in the tables: the R 1250 GS manuals up to 2021 list a maximum rear-wheel imbalance of 45 g; the April 2022 and later R 1250 GS editions and the R 1300 GS manuals list 5 g. That's a figure for the tire fitter, not for choosing tires.

Which manual editions does this cover?

BMW prints an edition date in each manual, not a model year. These are the editions we read:

  • R 1250 GS: May 2018, January 2019, June 2020, March 2021, April 2022; US-model editions March 2021 and April 2022.
  • R 1250 GS Adventure: June 2020, April 2022, April 2023; US-model editions March 2021 and April 2023.
  • R 1300 GS: February 2023, February 2024, February 2025; US-model editions February 2023 and February 2025.
  • R 1300 GS Adventure: February 2024.

All of them give the tire and pressure figures in the tables above. If your bike's manual is a different edition, use the figures in yours.

Which tires are approved?

BMW doesn't name tire models in these tables. The R 1250 GS manuals from 2018 to 2021 say: "An overview of currently approved tyres is available from your authorised BMW Motorrad Retailer or on the Internet at bmw-motorrad.com." Check that list, or ask a dealer, before you buy. Check that list, or ask a dealer, before you buy.

Can I fit a different size?

Only use a size approved by the motorcycle manufacturer or tire maker. Dunlop says a change should only be made "with the approval of the motorcycle or tire manufacturer."

Buying checklist for an R 1250 GS or R 1300 GS

  1. Confirm your model (GS or GS Adventure) and wheel type.
  2. Check the sizes against your manual or the tires fitted now: 120/70 R 19 front, 170/60 R 17 rear.
  3. Match or exceed load index 60 front and 72 rear, and speed category V.
  4. Check BMW's list of approved tires.
  5. Set cold pressures from your manual: 2.5 bar (36.3 psi) front, 2.9 bar (42.1 psi) rear in the manuals we read.

FAQ

What tire size does a BMW R 1250 GS take?

BMW's rider's manuals list 120/70 R 19 front and 170/60 R 17 rear, on 3.00" x 19" and 4.50" x 17" rims.

Does the R 1300 GS use the same tire sizes as the R 1250 GS?

Yes. BMW's R 1300 GS manuals list the same tire and rim sizes as the R 1250 GS manuals.

Does the GS Adventure use different tires?

Not in size. The GS Adventure manuals list the same sizes; what differs is the wheel type (cross-spoked, or forged on the R 1300 GS Adventure).

What tire pressure does BMW list for the R 1250 GS and R 1300 GS?

2.5 bar (36.3 psi) front and 2.9 bar (42.1 psi) rear, with cold tires, in the manuals we read. Check your own manual.
