BMW S 1000 RR Tire Sizes: What BMW's Rider's Manuals Say

March 22, 2026·Read article
❉ ShareFacebookCopy Link

BMW's rider's manuals for the S 1000 RR list 120/70 ZR 17 at the front on a 3.50" x 17" rim, and 190/55 ZR 17 at the rear on a 6.0" x 17" rim. With BMW's optional M carbon or M forged wheels, the rear tire listed is 200/55 ZR 17. The cold pressures are 2.5 bar (36.3 psi) front and 2.9 bar (42.1 psi) rear.

Every figure here comes from BMW Motorrad's own rider's manuals. We list the editions we read at the end, and what we couldn't find.

The speed category is from the 2020, 2021 and 2023 manuals, and the load index from the 2020, 2021 and 2023 manuals. The 2014 manual lists the same sizes and pressures.

How to read the sizes

  • 120/70 ZR 17: 120 mm nominal width, sidewall height 70% of the width, 17-inch rim.
  • 190/55 ZR 17: 190 mm nominal width, sidewall height 55% of the width, 17-inch rim.
  • 200/55 ZR 17: 200 mm nominal width, sidewall height 55% of the width, 17-inch rim.

190/55 or 200/55: which rear tire?

It depends on the wheels. In the manuals from 2020 onwards, standard aluminium cast wheels take 190/55 ZR 17, and BMW's optional M carbon wheels and M forged wheels take 200/55 ZR 17. The 2014 US-model manual lists 200/55 ZR 17 "with forged wheels."

The rear rim size is listed as 6.0" x 17" in every edition we read.

Don't swap between them without a dealer visit. BMW's May 2020 and April 2021 manuals say that when the rear tire size is changed from 190/55 ZR 17 to 200/55 ZR 17, "or vice versa, the parameters of the control systems have to be re-coded by a specialist workshop, preferably an authorised BMW Motorrad dealer." The 2014, 2020 and 2021 manuals we read include this note; we didn't find it in the 2023 editions.

Which manual editions does this cover?

BMW prints an edition date in each manual, not a model year. These are the English-language editions we read:

  • US model, September 2014 (1st edition)
  • May 2020 (2nd edition) and US model, May 2020 (2nd edition)
  • April 2021 (3rd edition)
  • April 2023 (2nd edition) and US model, April 2023 (2nd edition)

All six give the same front and rear sizes, rim sizes and cold pressures. We didn't find an English manual between the 2014 and 2020 editions, or one after April 2023. If your manual is a different edition, use the figures in yours.

Which tires are approved?

The manuals don't name tire models. The May 2020 manual says an overview of currently approved tyres "is available from your authorised BMW Motorrad Retailer or on the Internet at bmw-motorrad.com."

Can I fit a different size?

Only use a size approved by the motorcycle manufacturer or tire maker. Dunlop says a change should only be made "with the approval of the motorcycle or tire manufacturer." On the S 1000 RR, a change between the 190/55 and 200/55 rear also needs the control systems re-coded, as above.

Buying checklist for an S 1000 RR

  1. Check which wheels your bike has: standard cast, M carbon or M forged.
  2. Match the rear size to the wheel: 190/55 ZR 17 on cast wheels, 200/55 ZR 17 on M carbon or M forged wheels, in the manuals we read.
  3. Front: 120/70 ZR 17.
  4. Match or exceed load index 58 front and 75 rear, and speed category W.
  5. Check BMW's approved tire list, and set cold pressures from your manual.

FAQ

What tire size does a BMW S 1000 RR take?

BMW's rider's manuals list 120/70 ZR 17 front and 190/55 ZR 17 rear, or 200/55 ZR 17 rear with M carbon or M forged wheels.

Can I put a 200/55 rear tire on an S 1000 RR with cast wheels?

BMW's manuals list 190/55 ZR 17 for the standard cast wheels. They also say that changing between 190/55 and 200/55 needs the control systems re-coded by a specialist workshop.

What tire pressure does BMW list for the S 1000 RR?

2.5 bar (36.3 psi) front and 2.9 bar (42.1 psi) rear, with cold tires, in the manuals we read.

What rim sizes does the S 1000 RR have?

3.50" x 17" front and 6.0" x 17" rear, according to BMW's manuals.
