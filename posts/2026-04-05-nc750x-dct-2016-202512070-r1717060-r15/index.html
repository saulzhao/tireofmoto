Honda NC750X and NC750X DCT Tire Sizes: 2027 Model

April 5, 2026 · By Zenyare Tire
𝕏 Share Facebook Copy Link

Honda UK lists 120/70-ZR17M/C (58W) front and 160/60-ZR17M/C (69W) rear for the 2027 NC750X and NC750X DCT. Both wheels are 17-inch multi-spoke cast aluminium: MT3.50 front and MT4.50 rear on the manual-gearbox model.

These figures come from Honda UK's specification page for the 2027 model. We didn't find official Honda data for earlier years, so if your NC750X is older, check your owner's manual.

Tire and rim sizes (2027 model, Honda UK)

Rear wheel17 M/C X MT4.50, multi-spoke cast aluminiumMulti-spoke cast aluminium

Honda's page lists the same tire sizes for both versions. For the DCT, it gives the wheel type but not the rim width.

How to read the sizes

  • 120/70-ZR17: 120 mm nominal width, sidewall height 70% of the width, 17-inch rim.
  • 160/60-ZR17: 160 mm nominal width, sidewall height 60% of the width, 17-inch rim.
  • (58W) and (69W): as printed by Honda. When you replace tires, follow your owner's manual for the markings to match.

What about tire pressure?

Honda UK's specification page doesn't list tire pressures. Use the figures in your owner's manual or on the tire information label on your bike.

Is the DCT different?

Not in tire size. Honda UK lists the same front and rear sizes for the NC750X and the NC750X DCT.

Can I fit a different size?

Only use a size approved by the motorcycle manufacturer or tire maker. Dunlop says a change should only be made "with the approval of the motorcycle or tire manufacturer."

Buying checklist for an NC750X

  1. Check the size on your current tires' sidewalls: 120/70-ZR17 front and 160/60-ZR17 rear on the 2027 model.
  2. If your bike is an older model, confirm the sizes in your owner's manual.
  3. Match the markings your owner's manual specifies.
  4. Set cold pressures from your manual or the label on the bike.

FAQ

What tire size does the Honda NC750X take?

Honda UK lists 120/70-ZR17 front and 160/60-ZR17 rear for the 2027 NC750X and NC750X DCT.

Does the NC750X DCT use different tires?

No. Honda UK lists the same sizes for both.

What rim sizes does the NC750X have?

Honda UK lists 17M/C X MT3.50 front and 17 M/C X MT4.50 rear for the 2027 manual-gearbox model.
