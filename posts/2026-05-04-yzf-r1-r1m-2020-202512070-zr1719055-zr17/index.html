Yamaha YZF-R1 (2020, 2022–2025) and R1M (2023–2025) Tire Sizes

Published on May 4, 2026

The most important thing to know about R1 tires is that the R1 and the R1M don't share a rear size. Yamaha lists a 190/55ZR17 rear for the standard R1 and a 200/55ZR17 rear for the R1M. Buying for one from a listing written for the other is an easy mistake.

This page sets out what Yamaha publishes for each model and year we could find, and what you still need to check on your own bike. We only list a figure when it appears in an official Yamaha document. Anything we could not source is left out.

The European sheets give the load index and speed symbol (58W front, 75W rear for the R1). The US pages give the size without them. We didn't find a Yamaha document giving the R1M's load index.

How to read these numbers

  • 120/70ZR17: 120 mm nominal section width, sidewall height 70% of the width, ZR speed-category marking, 17-inch rim.
  • 190/55ZR17 (R1): 190 mm nominal width, sidewall 55% of the width. On the European sheets the rear is (75W): load index 75, speed symbol W.
  • 200/55ZR17 (R1M): 200 mm nominal width, sidewall 55% of the width. That's 10 mm wider than the R1's rear at the same aspect ratio.

Original-equipment tires listed by Yamaha

  • Yamaha USA lists Bridgestone BATTLAX RACING STREET RS11F (front) and RS11R (rear) on both the R1 and the R1M for 2023, 2024 and 2025.
  • Yamaha Motor Europe's 2020 sheet lists "Latest Bridgestone Battlax RS11 tyres" among the R1's features; the 2022 and 2024 sheets also mention RS11 tyres.

The fitted tire can vary by market. These are the tires Yamaha lists, not a recommendation from us.

R1 vs R1M: what else differs

On Yamaha USA's 2024 pages, the R1 has a 43 mm KYB inverted fork and KYB rear shock, while the R1M has Öhlins Electronic Racing Suspension front and rear. Wet weight is listed as 448 lb for the R1 and 450 lb for the R1M. Both list a "Brake Control System and ABS" at the front and rear.

For tires, the difference that matters is the rear: 190/55ZR17 on the R1, 200/55ZR17 on the R1M.

Brakes and ABS

Yamaha Motor Europe's 2020 sheet lists a "2-Mode Brake Control (BC)" system for the R1. Yamaha USA's 2023–2025 pages for both models list "Brake Control System and ABS" for the front and rear brakes.

Which years and markets does this cover?

  • YZF-R1 2020, 2022, 2024: Yamaha Motor Europe spec sheets (UK English), European models.
  • YZF-R1 2023, 2024, 2025 and YZF-R1M 2023, 2024, 2025: Yamaha Motor Corporation USA specification pages, US models.
  • YZF-R1 2021: the Yamaha Motor Europe sheet returned an access error, and we found no US page for that year.
  • YZF-R1M 2020, 2021, 2022: we did not find a Yamaha document we could read.

If your bike isn't in the table, read the tire information in your owner's manual or on the bike. That's written for your exact model and market.

Rim width and tire pressure

Neither Yamaha's European sheets nor its US spec pages list rim widths or tire pressures, and we didn't find an official owner's manual we could read online. Use the pressures in your owner's manual or on the label on the bike. Don't take a pressure from a forum or another model.

Can I fit a different size?

Only use a size approved by the motorcycle manufacturer or tire maker. Dunlop says a change should only be made "with the approval of the motorcycle or tire manufacturer."

That includes putting the R1M's 200/55 rear on a standard R1, or the R1's 190/55 rear on an R1M. Yamaha lists different sizes for the two models, so don't swap them without approval.

Buying checklist for an R1 or R1M

  1. Confirm whether you have the R1 or the R1M, and the model year and market.
  2. Match the rear size to the model: 190/55ZR17 for the R1, 200/55ZR17 for the R1M (per the Yamaha documents above).
  3. Read the load index and speed symbol from your owner's manual or the tire label, and never go below them.
  4. Check the manufacture date on any tire you buy (the last four digits of the DOT code show week and year) and follow the tire maker's guidance on age.
  5. Replace the tire when the tread reaches the wear indicator bars, or earlier if it is damaged.

FAQ

What tire size does a Yamaha YZF-R1 take?

Yamaha lists 120/70ZR17 front and 190/55ZR17 rear for the R1 in 2020 and 2022–2025. The European sheets add 58W front and 75W rear.

Is the R1M rear tire the same as the R1?

No. Yamaha USA lists a 200/55ZR17 rear for the R1M (2023–2025) and a 190/55ZR17 rear for the R1.

What tires does Yamaha fit to the R1?

Yamaha USA lists Bridgestone BATTLAX RACING STREET RS11F and RS11R for both the R1 and R1M in 2023–2025. Fitted tires can vary by market.

Can I use different tire sizes?

Only use a size approved by the motorcycle manufacturer or tire maker. Dunlop says a change should only be made "with the approval of the motorcycle or tire manufacturer."

What tire pressure should I run?

Use the figures in your owner's manual or on the label on the bike. Yamaha's spec sheets and pages don't list pressures.
