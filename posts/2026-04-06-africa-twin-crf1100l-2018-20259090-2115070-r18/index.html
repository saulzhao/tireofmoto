Honda Africa Twin CRF1100L Tire Sizes: What Honda's Own Documents Say

April 6, 2026 · By Zenyare Tire
𝕏 Share Facebook Copy Link

Honda lists 90/90-21 front and 150/70R18 rear for the CRF1100L Africa Twin, on a 21-inch front and 18-inch rear wire-spoke wheel. The Adventure Sports used the same sizes in Honda's 2020 brochure, but Honda UK's current specifications list a 110/80R19 front for it, and the 2025 owner's manual lists the same size for its A4, D4 and DL4 codes.

Every figure here comes from three Honda documents: the UK 2020 brochure, Honda Motor Europe's 2025 owner's manual, and Honda UK's current specification pages. We didn't find official Honda data for 2021–2024, so check your own owner's manual if your bike is from those years.

Two differences stand out between these documents:

  • The 2020 standard model was tube type; the current standard model is tubeless. Honda's 2020 UK brochure lists the standard Africa Twin's tires as "tube type" and the Adventure Sports' as "tubeless type."
  • The Adventure Sports front went from 21 to 19 inches. The 2020 brochure lists 90/90-21; Honda UK's current specifications list 110/80-R19 on a 19-inch rim.

What the 2025 owner's manual lists

Honda Motor Europe's 2025 owner's manual covers several versions, by code. It doesn't name them, so we give the codes as printed:

Manual codesFront tireRear tire
CRF1100A / A3 / D / D390/90-21M/C 54H, bias-ply, tubeless150/70R18M/C 70H, radial, tubeless
CRF1100A4 / D4 / DL4110/80R19M/C 59V, radial, tubeless150/70R18M/C 70H, radial, tubeless

The 110/80R19 front on the A4, D4 and DL4 codes matches the Adventure Sports in Honda UK's current specifications. The manual also lists M+S tyres for its "ED, II ED, III ED" types, with R speed ratings (90/90-21M/C 54R, 110/80R19M/C 59R, 150/70R18M/C 70R), and says that with M+S tires fitted, maximum speed should be kept "to less than 160 km/h (99 mph)."

The same figures are printed on the tire information label shown in the manual. The 2020 brochure and Honda UK's current specification pages don't list pressures. Use the label on your bike or your own manual.

Can I fit a tube?

Not on a bike set up for tubeless tires. The 2025 owner's manual says:

  • "Do not install a tube inside a tubeless tyre on this vehicle. Excessive heat build-up can cause the tube to burst."
  • "Use only tubeless tyres on this vehicle. The rims are designed for tubeless tyres, and during hard acceleration or braking, a tube-type tyre could slip on the rim and cause the tyre to rapidly deflate."

The 2020 standard model was listed with tube-type tires, so these warnings come from the 2025 manual only. If your bike is a 2020–2024 model, check your manual and the markings on your rims and tires.

What does Honda say about replacement tires?

The 2025 manual says to "use the recommended tyres or their equivalents of the same size, construction, speed rating, and load range," and to have the wheel balanced after the tire is installed. It also says: "Have your tyres replaced by your dealer."

The manual names recommended tire models for each version. Check the list in your own manual.

Can I fit a different size?

Only use a size approved by the motorcycle manufacturer or tire maker. Dunlop says a change should only be made "with the approval of the motorcycle or tire manufacturer." On the Africa Twin, note that the standard model and the Adventure Sports use different front sizes in Honda's current data.

Buying checklist for an Africa Twin

  1. Find your model and year: standard Africa Twin or Adventure Sports.
  2. Check the front size on your tire's sidewall: 90/90-21 or 110/80R19.
  3. Check whether your bike runs tube-type or tubeless tires (your manual and the rim and tire markings).
  4. Match the size, construction, speed rating and load range in your manual.
  5. Set cold pressures from your manual or the label on the bike.

FAQ

What tire size does the Honda Africa Twin CRF1100L take?

Honda lists 90/90-21 front and 150/70R18 rear for the standard model. The current Adventure Sports uses 110/80R19 front and 150/70R18 rear.

Is the Africa Twin tubeless?

Honda's 2025 manual and current UK specifications list tubeless tires. Honda's 2020 UK brochure listed the standard model with tube-type tires and the Adventure Sports with tubeless tires.

What tire pressure does Honda list for the Africa Twin?

The 2025 owner's manual lists 225 kPa (33 psi) front and 250 kPa (36 psi) rear solo, and 280 kPa (41 psi) rear with a passenger, cold.

Did the Adventure Sports change front wheel size?

Yes. Honda's 2020 brochure lists a 21-inch front; Honda UK's current specifications list a 19-inch front with 110/80R19.
