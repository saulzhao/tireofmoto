Yamaha Ténéré 700 and World Raid Tire Sizes: What Yamaha Publishes

Published on June 9, 2026

Yamaha Motor Europe's spec sheets list the same tire sizes for every Ténéré 700 from 2019 to 2025 and every Ténéré 700 World Raid from 2022 to 2024: a 90/90-21 front with load index 54 and speed symbol V, and a 150/70 R18 rear with load index 70 and speed symbol V. The standard Ténéré 700 sheets describe spoke wheels. What does change between years is how the size is written, the suspension, and the weight, and the World Raid differs from the standard bike in several of those.

We only list a figure when it appears in an official Yamaha document. Anything we couldn't source is left out.

The 2019 and 2020 sheets add that the wheels are spoke wheels "with Pirelli Scorpion Rally STR" tires. The later sheets don't name a tire.

How to read these numbers

  • 90/90-21 54V: 90 mm nominal section width, sidewall height 90% of the width, 21-inch rim. 54 is the load index and V the speed symbol.
  • 150/70 R18 70V: 150 mm nominal width, sidewall 70% of the width, 18-inch rim, R for radial construction; load index 70, speed symbol V.
  • M/C means motorcycle. M+S is a mud and snow marking.

Notice that the 2019–2020 sheets write the front as "90/90 R21", while the sheets from 2021 write "90/90 - 21". The sheets don't explain the change, so we can't tell you whether it reflects a different tire or only a different way of writing the line. Read the sidewall of the tire on your bike.

When you buy, match the load index and speed symbol Yamaha lists, and don't go below them.

The 2019 and 2020 sheets don't list caster angle and trail. From 2021 every sheet lists 27° and 105 mm.

The 2025 sheet lists standard and "Low" versions of the suspension travel, seat height and ground clearance, and describes "Switchable ABS and TCS at the press of a button". The tire lines are the same as 2021–2024.

Which years and markets does this cover?

  • Ténéré 700, 2019–2025: Yamaha Motor Europe spec sheets, UK English. These describe the European model.
  • World Raid, 2022–2024: the same source. We didn't find a 2025 World Raid sheet in any of the seven language versions we tried, so this page doesn't cover a 2025 World Raid.
  • Rally Edition 2020–2023 and World Rally 2023: the same source.
  • US models: we couldn't open Yamaha USA's Ténéré 700 spec page, so this page doesn't give US figures. Specifications can differ by market. Use your owner's manual or the tire label.

Rim width, tire pressure and inner tubes

Yamaha's spec sheets don't list rim widths, tire pressures or whether the wheels take inner tubes, and we didn't find an official owner's manual we could open online. Use the figures in your owner's manual or on the label on the bike, and read the tire sidewall for TT (tube type) or TL (tubeless). Don't use a figure from a forum or another model.

Can I fit a different size?

Only use a size approved by the motorcycle manufacturer or tire maker. Dunlop says a change should only be made "with the approval of the motorcycle or tire manufacturer."

Every Yamaha Europe sheet listed above gives a 21-inch front and an 18-inch rear.

Buying checklist for a Ténéré 700 or World Raid

  1. Confirm your model, model year and market.
  2. For a European bike covered above, the sizes are 90/90-21 54V front and 150/70 R18 70V rear. For any other bike, read your owner's manual or the tire label.
  3. Match the load index and speed symbol, and never go below them.
  4. Check whether your wheels take an inner tube before you order.
  5. Check the manufacture date on any tire you buy and follow the tire maker's guidance on age.

FAQ

What tire size does a Yamaha Ténéré 700 take?

Yamaha Motor Europe's spec sheets for 2019 to 2025 list a 90/90-21 front (54V) and a 150/70 R18 rear (70V). The 2019 and 2020 sheets write the front as 90/90 R21.

Does the World Raid use different tires from the standard Ténéré 700?

Not in size. The 2022–2024 World Raid sheets list the same 90/90-21 54V front and 150/70 R18 70V rear. The World Raid has longer wheel travel, bigger fuel tanks and more weight.

What tires did the Ténéré 700 come with?

The 2019 and 2020 sheets name Pirelli Scorpion Rally STR. The sheets from 2021 don't name a tire.

What tire pressure should I run?

Use the figures in your owner's manual or on the label on the bike. Yamaha's spec sheets don't list pressures.
