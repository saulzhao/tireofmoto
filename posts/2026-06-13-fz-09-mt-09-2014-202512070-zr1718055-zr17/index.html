Yamaha MT-09 2014–2025 Tire Sizes: What Yamaha Publishes

Published on June 13, 2026

The MT-09 has changed through several engine and chassis updates since 2014, but its tire sizes have stayed the same. The size is only part of the answer, though: the load index and speed symbol matter when you buy, and the rider aids and suspension changed from one generation to the next. This page sets out what Yamaha's own European spec sheets say for each model year from 2014 to 2025, and what you still need to check on your bike.

We only list a figure when it appears in an official Yamaha document. Anything we couldn't source is left out.

Tire sizes by model year

Yamaha Motor Europe publishes a spec sheet for each model year. These are the tire entries for the MT-09:

Model yearsFront tireRear tire

2021–2025120/70 ZR17M/C (58W) Tubeless180/55 ZR17M/C (73W) Tubeless

Source: Yamaha Motor Europe, MT-09 spec sheets (UK English), one for each year from 2014 to 2025. All 12 sheets give the same sizes, load indexes and speed symbols. Only the punctuation of the line changes.

How to read these numbers

  • 120/70 ZR17 (58W): 120 mm nominal section width, sidewall height 70% of the width, ZR speed-category marking, 17-inch rim. 58 is the load index and W the speed symbol.
  • 180/55 ZR17 (73W): 180 mm nominal width, sidewall 55% of the width, 17-inch rim; load index 73, speed symbol W.
  • M/C means motorcycle, and every sheet marks both tires as tubeless.

When you buy, match the load index and speed symbol Yamaha lists, and don't go below them.

The tire line stayed the same, but the spec sheets show three clear stages:

A few notes from the sheets:

  • 2016: the sheet lists a Traction Control System among the features.
  • 2017: Yamaha mentions "fully adjustable front forks", a Quick Shift System, an assist and slipper clutch and "switchable TCS", and says the "Traction Control System (TCS) and ABS intervene when required".
  • 2021: the 890 cc model gets a "6-axis IMU" and "lean-sensitive" rider aids, including a 3-mode TCS.
  • 2024: the sheet lists an upside-down 41 mm fork and two transmission versions, standard 6-speed manual and Y-AMT.

None of these changes came with a different tire size in Yamaha's sheets.

Which years and markets does this cover?

  • 2014–2025: Yamaha Motor Europe spec sheets, UK English. These describe the European model.
  • FZ-09: in the US, the bike was sold as the FZ-09 for some years. We couldn't open Yamaha USA's FZ-09 or MT-09 spec pages, so this page doesn't give figures for the US model.
  • Other markets: specifications can differ by market. Read the tire information in your owner's manual or on the bike.

Rim width and tire pressure

Yamaha's spec sheets don't list rim widths or tire pressures, and we didn't find an official MT-09 owner's manual we could open online. Use the pressures in your owner's manual or on the label on the bike. Don't use a figure from a forum or another model.

Can I fit a different size?

Only use a size approved by the motorcycle manufacturer or tire maker. Dunlop says a change should only be made "with the approval of the motorcycle or tire manufacturer."

That applies to common questions such as fitting a wider rear or a different profile. Yamaha's sheets list 180/55 ZR17 for every year from 2014 to 2025.

Buying checklist for an MT-09

  1. Confirm your model year and market.
  2. For a European 2014–2025 bike, the sizes are 120/70 ZR17 (58W) front and 180/55 ZR17 (73W) rear, tubeless. For any other bike, read your owner's manual or the tire label.
  3. Match the load index and speed symbol, and never go below them.
  4. Check the manufacture date on any tire you buy (the last four digits of the DOT code show week and year) and follow the tire maker's guidance on age.
  5. Replace the tire when the tread reaches the wear indicator bars, or earlier if it's damaged.

FAQ

What tire size does a Yamaha MT-09 take?

Yamaha Motor Europe's spec sheets for every model year from 2014 to 2025 list 120/70 ZR17M/C (58W) front and 180/55 ZR17M/C (73W) rear, both tubeless.

Did the 890 cc MT-09 change tire size?

No. The 2021 sheet, the first with the 890 cc engine, lists the same sizes, load indexes and speed symbols as 2014.

What load index does the MT-09 need?

Yamaha lists 58 at the front and 73 at the rear, both with speed symbol W.

Can I use different tire sizes?

Only use a size approved by the motorcycle manufacturer or tire maker. Dunlop says a change should only be made "with the approval of the motorcycle or tire manufacturer."

What tire pressure should I run?

Use the figures in your owner's manual or on the label on the bike. Yamaha's spec sheets don't list pressures.
