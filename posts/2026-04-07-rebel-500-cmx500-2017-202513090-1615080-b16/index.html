Honda Rebel 500 / CMX500 Tire Sizes by Year (2017–2024)

April 7, 2026 · By Zenyare Tire
𝕏 Share Facebook Copy Link

Honda lists a 130/90-16 front and 150/80-16 rear for the Rebel 500 (sold in Europe as the CMX500 Rebel). American Honda's specifications give the same two sizes for every year we checked from 2017 to 2024, for both the ABS and non-ABS models, and Honda's current UK and Australian specifications list the same sizes.

We didn't find an official Honda specification for the 2021, 2025 or 2026 model years, so if your Rebel is from one of those years, check your owner's manual or the tires on the bike.

Each row is copied as Honda printed it. American Honda's tables give one tire size for all the Rebel 500 versions listed that year, so the ABS and non-ABS models share the same sizes in those specs. Only Honda Europe's 2017 release prints the load and speed marking (67H front, 71H rear), and it notes that "All specifications are provisional and subject to change without notice." Honda UK's current page writes the sizes as "130/90/R16" and "150/80/R16".

Honda Europe's 2017 release also says: "Dunlop tyres are fitted as standard sized 130/90-16 front and 150/80-16 rear."

The two Honda documents give different rear rim widths: MT3.00 in the 2017 launch specification and MT3.50 on the current UK page. We've copied both as printed. American Honda's specs don't list rim widths. If you're buying a rear tire, confirm the rim size for your model year in your owner's manual.

How to read the sizes

  • 130/90-16: 130 mm nominal width, sidewall height 90% of the width, 16-inch rim.
  • 150/80-16: 150 mm nominal width, sidewall height 80% of the width, 16-inch rim.
  • 67H and 71H: as printed in Honda Europe's 2017 specification. When you replace tires, follow your owner's manual for the markings to match.

Do ABS and non-ABS Rebel 500s use different tires?

Not in American Honda's specifications. Each year's table from 2017 to 2024 lists the ABS and non-ABS Rebel 500 (and the ABS SE from 2022) with one front size and one rear size.

What about tire pressure?

None of the Honda specifications we read lists tire pressures. Use the figures in your owner's manual or on the tire information label on your bike.

Can I fit a different size?

Only use a size approved by the motorcycle manufacturer or tire maker. Dunlop says a change should only be made "with the approval of the motorcycle or tire manufacturer."

Buying checklist for a Rebel 500

  1. Check the size on your current tires' sidewalls: 130/90-16 front and 150/80-16 rear in Honda's specs.
  2. If your bike is a 2021, 2025 or 2026 model, confirm the sizes in your owner's manual.
  3. Match the load and speed markings your owner's manual specifies.
  4. Set cold pressures from your manual or the label on the bike.

FAQ

What tire size does the Honda Rebel 500 take?

Honda lists 130/90-16 front and 150/80-16 rear in American Honda's 2017–2024 specifications and in Honda's current UK and Australian specifications.

What rim sizes does the Rebel 500 have?

Honda UK lists 16M/C x MT3.00 front and 16M/C x MT3.50 rear for the current CMX500 Rebel E-clutch. Honda Europe's 2017 specification lists MT3.00 for both.

Is the CMX500 Rebel the same size as the US Rebel 500?

In the specs we checked, yes: Honda Europe, Honda UK and Honda Australia list the same 130/90 front and 150/80 rear 16-inch sizes as American Honda.
