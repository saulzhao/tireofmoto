Replacement Tips

  1. Always match OEM tire size or consult a tire professional before deviating.
  2. Check the DOT manufacture date (last 4 digits on sidewall) - avoid tires older than 3 years.
  3. Sport bikes: replace rear tires every 5,000-10,000 miles; touring tires can last 20,000+ miles.

Recommended Brands

  • Premium: Michelin, Bridgestone, Pirelli, Dunlop, Metzeler
  • Mid-range: Toyo, IRC, Wulfsport, Shinko
  • Budget: Avon, Mitas, Kumho (where available)

FAQ

Can I use different tire sizes?

Minor deviations are possible but can affect ABS calibration. Consult the tire manufacturer's fitment guide.

How often should tires be replaced?

Every 3-5 years by age or when tread reaches wear bars, whichever comes first.
