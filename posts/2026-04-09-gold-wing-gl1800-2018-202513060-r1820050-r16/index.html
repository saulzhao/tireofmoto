Honda Gold Wing GL1800 Tire Sizes by Year (2018–2024)

April 9, 2026 · By Zenyare Tire
𝕏 Share Facebook Copy Link

Honda lists a 130/70-18 front and 200/55-16 rear for the current-generation Gold Wing (GL1800). American Honda's specifications give those sizes for every version listed in each year's specification from 2018 to 2024, and Honda's current UK and Australian specifications list the same sizes.

We didn't find an official Honda specification for the 2025 US model year, so if your Gold Wing is a 2025, check your owner's manual or the tires on the bike.

Each row is copied as Honda printed it, including the model names, which change slightly between years. American Honda's tables give one front and one rear size for all the Gold Wing versions listed that year, so the Tour, Airbag, DCT and base models share the same tire sizes in those specs.

American Honda writes the rear as "200/55-R16" in its 2018 to 2021 specs and "200/55-16" from 2022. That's a difference in how the size is printed; the numbers are the same. Only Honda UK's current page prints the load and speed marking: 63H front and 77H rear.

The model codes (GL1800DAP and so on) appear only in American Honda's 2023 and 2024 specs, so we haven't applied them to earlier years.

Rim sizes

The two Honda documents agree: 3.5-inch front and 6.0-inch rear rim widths. American Honda's specs don't list rim widths. Honda Europe's 22YM specification notes that "All specifications are provisional and subject to change without notice."

How to read the sizes

  • 130/70-18 (130/70R18): 130 mm nominal width, sidewall height 70% of the width, 18-inch rim.
  • 200/55-16 (200/55R16): 200 mm nominal width, sidewall height 55% of the width, 16-inch rim.
  • 63H and 77H: as printed on Honda UK's current page. When you replace tires, follow your owner's manual for the markings to match.

Do the Gold Wing and Gold Wing Tour use different tires?

Not in American Honda's specifications. From 2018 to 2024, each year's table lists one front size and one rear size for every model, Tour or not, airbag or not, DCT or manual.

Tire pressure monitoring (TPMS)

Honda Europe's 22YM Gold Wing Tour release says: "Tyre pressure is displayed as a numerical figure in the bottom left area of the instruments."

For the base model, Honda Europe's 23YM release says: "An additional safety-focussed upgrade for 2023 for the GL1800 Gold Wing comes in the shape of a new Tyre Pressure Monitoring System (TPMS) integrated into the 7-inch full-colour TFT liquid crystal display, which has been inherited from the GL1800 Gold Wing Tour." It adds that the TPMS "will illuminate a clear and bright warning light if pressure becomes sub-optimal."

Honda UK's current page lists "Tyre pressure control" in the equipment of both the Gold Wing Tour and the Gold Wing.

These are European and UK documents. We haven't confirmed which US model years have TPMS from American Honda's own specs, so check your owner's manual.

What about tire pressure and load?

None of the Honda specifications we read lists tire pressures or a load limit. The Gold Wing is often ridden two-up with luggage, so use the pressures and load information in your owner's manual or on the labels on your bike.

Can I fit a different size?

Only use a size approved by the motorcycle manufacturer or tire maker. Dunlop says a change should only be made "with the approval of the motorcycle or tire manufacturer."

Buying checklist for a Gold Wing GL1800

  1. Check the size on your current tires' sidewalls: 130/70-18 front and 200/55-16 rear in Honda's specs.
  2. If your bike is a 2025 model, confirm the sizes in your owner's manual.
  3. Match the load and speed markings your owner's manual specifies.
  4. Set cold pressures from your manual or the label on the bike, especially if you ride with a passenger or luggage.

FAQ

What tire size does the Honda Gold Wing GL1800 take?

Honda lists 130/70-18 front and 200/55-16 rear for every 2018–2024 Gold Wing in American Honda's specs, and the same sizes on its current UK and Australian pages.

Does the Gold Wing Tour use the same tires as the base Gold Wing?

Yes, in American Honda's 2018–2024 specifications every version shares one front and one rear size.

What rim sizes does the Gold Wing have?

Honda UK lists 18M/C x MT3.50 front and 16M/C x MT6.00 rear for the current Gold Wing Tour. Honda Europe's 22YM specification gives the same widths.

Does the Gold Wing have tire pressure monitoring?

Honda Europe says the 22YM Gold Wing Tour shows tire pressure on the instruments, and that the base Gold Wing gained TPMS for 2023. Check your owner's manual for your model and market.
