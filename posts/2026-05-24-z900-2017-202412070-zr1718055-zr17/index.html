Kawasaki Z900 2019–2024 Tire Sizes: What Kawasaki Publishes

Published on May 24, 2026

Kawasaki's US spec sheets list the same tire sizes for the Z900 from 2019 to 2024: 120/70 ZR17 at the front and 180/55 ZR17 at the rear. The sheets don't give a load index or a speed symbol. Read those from the tires on your bike or from your owner's manual. The tires didn't change, but the 2020 sheet adds traction control, power modes and a TFT display, and the 2021 sheet changes rake and trail.

We only list a figure when it appears in an official Kawasaki document. Anything we couldn't source is left out.

How to read these numbers

  • 120/70 ZR17: 120 mm nominal section width, sidewall height 70% of the width, 17-inch rim.
  • 180/55 ZR17: 180 mm nominal width, sidewall 55% of the width, 17-inch rim.

That's all the sheets give. A replacement tire also carries a load index and a speed symbol, which the sheets don't list. Take them from the tires fitted to your bike or from your owner's manual, and don't go below them.

What about 2017 and 2018?

The current page title starts at 2017. We couldn't read Kawasaki's own figures for 2017 and 2018. The 2017 and 2018 Z900 ABS spec sheets download from Kawasaki's site, but their text can't be extracted, and we found no non-ABS sheets for those years. So this page doesn't state sizes for 2017–2018. For a bike from those years, use the owner's manual or the size printed on the tires fitted at the factory.

A few notes from the sheets:

  • 2020: the sheet adds the rider aids above, plus "Rideology the App Smartphone Connectivity" and "TFT Instrumentation" under special features. The 2019 sheet lists neither.
  • 2021: rake and trail change from 24.5° / 4.1 in to 24.9° / 4.3 in. The sheets don't say why.
  • 2021 non-ABS: Kawasaki's 2021 Z900 sheet (without ABS in the name) lists the same tire sizes and the same ABS brake wording as the ABS sheet.
  • 2023–2024: the front suspension line mentions rebound damping adjustment only. The earlier sheets also mention spring preload. The sheets don't say whether the fork itself changed.

ABS and non-ABS

Most sheets are for the ABS model. The 2019 sheet gives curb weights for both versions, and the 2024 sheet header gives a price for the non-ABS model. The tire sizes on every sheet we read are the same. Kawasaki's sheets don't show different tire sizes for ABS and non-ABS bikes.

Which markets does this cover?

The sheets come from Kawasaki USA and describe US models. Specifications can differ by market. If your bike was sold elsewhere, use your owner's manual or the tire label.

Rim width and tire pressure

Kawasaki's spec sheets don't list rim widths or tire pressures. The owner's manual is the place to look, and Kawasaki's Owners Center asks for the bike's details before it shows the manual. Use the pressures in your owner's manual or on the label on the bike. Don't use a figure from a forum, a different model year or another model.

Can I fit a different size?

Only use a size approved by the motorcycle manufacturer or tire maker. Dunlop says a change should only be made "with the approval of the motorcycle or tire manufacturer."

That applies to common questions such as fitting a 190-section rear. Kawasaki's sheets list 180/55 ZR17 for every Z900 sheet we read from 2019 to 2024.

Buying checklist for a Z900

  1. Confirm your model year, market and whether your bike has ABS.
  2. For a US 2019–2024 bike, the sizes are 120/70 ZR17 front and 180/55 ZR17 rear. For any other bike, read your owner's manual or the tire label.
  3. Take the load index and speed symbol from your owner's manual or the current tires, and never go below them.
  4. Follow the tire maker's guidance on tire age.
  5. Follow your owner's manual and the tire maker's guidance on when to replace a tire.

FAQ

What tire size does a 2019–2024 Kawasaki Z900 take?

Kawasaki's US spec sheets list 120/70 ZR17 front and 180/55 ZR17 rear for every year from 2019 to 2024.

Did the 2020 Z900 change tire size?

No. The 2020 sheet keeps the same sizes. It adds traction control, two power modes, integrated riding modes and a TFT display.

Does the non-ABS Z900 use different tires?

Not on Kawasaki's sheets. The 2019 sheet gives a non-ABS curb weight, and the 2021 Z900 sheet (without ABS in its name) lists the same 120/70 ZR17 and 180/55 ZR17.

What load index, speed rating and tire pressure does the Z900 need?

Kawasaki's spec sheets don't list them. Use the figures in your owner's manual, on the label on the bike, or on the tires fitted to your bike.
