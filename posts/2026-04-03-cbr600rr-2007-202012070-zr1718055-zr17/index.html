Honda CBR600RR 2013 Tire Sizes: What Honda Publishes

Published on April 3, 2026

This page covers the 2013 Honda CBR600RR, because that's the model year for which we found Honda's own published wheel and tire specification. It shows the sizes Honda states, the details it gives about wheels and ABS, and what you still need to read off your own bike or owner's manual.

We only list a figure here when we could find it in an official Honda document. Anything we could not source is left out, so you know what to check yourself.

Front and rear tire sizes from Honda

Honda Motor Europe's press pack for the 2013 CBR600RR (released 12 November 2012) gives this wheel and tire specification:

ItemFrontRear
Wheel typeHollow section 12-spoke cast aluminiumHollow section 12-spoke cast aluminium
Rim size17M/C x MT3.517M/C x MT5.5
Tire size120/70-ZR17M/C180/55-ZR17M/C

Source: Honda Motor Europe, "2013 HONDA CBR600RR" press pack, technical specifications.

How to read these numbers

  • 120/70-ZR17: 120 mm nominal section width, a sidewall height of 70% of that width, a ZR speed-category marking, and a 17-inch rim diameter.
  • 180/55-ZR17: 180 mm nominal width with a sidewall height of 55% of the width, on a 17-inch rim.
  • MT3.5 / MT5.5: the rim width in inches (3.5 in front, 5.5 in rear). "M/C" marks a motorcycle fitment.

The press pack is a launch document for the 2013 European model. It confirms that model year; it doesn't confirm other years.

What Honda says about the 2013 model

  • New wheels. The press pack describes the 2013 update as including "12-spoke wheels", similar in design to the CBR1000RR Fireblade's.
  • ABS. It says Honda announced its electronic Combined ABS in June 2008, "available on both the CBR600RR and CBR1000RR Fireblade in 2009." For 2013 it lists the ABS system type as Honda Electronic combined ABS.
  • Weight. It gives a kerb weight of 196 kg with electronic Combined ABS.

If your CBR600RR isn't a 2013

We did not find an official Honda specification page or owner's manual for other model years that we could read and cite. The tire sizes may well be the same, but we are not going to state that without Honda's own text.

For any other year, the quickest check is the tire information label on the bike or the specifications section of your owner's manual. Both are written for your exact model and market.

Tire pressure

Honda's press pack does not give tire pressures, and we did not find an official CBR600RR owner's manual we could read online. Use the pressures on the label on your bike or in your owner's manual. Do not take a pressure figure from a forum or another model.

Load index and speed rating

The 2013 press pack prints "(58W)" after both the front and rear tire sizes. A 58 load index on the 180/55-ZR17 rear does not match what we would expect for that size, so we are not repeating it as a recommendation until we can check it against the owner's manual. When you buy tires, match the load index and speed symbol printed in your owner's manual or on the tire information label, and never go below them.

Can I fit a different size?

Only use a size approved by the motorcycle manufacturer or tire maker. Dunlop says a change should only be made "with the approval of the motorcycle or tire manufacturer."

That covers the common questions, such as going from a 180/55 to a 190/50 rear, or changing the rim width. The tire size and the rim width have to suit each other, and that pairing is set by Honda and the tire maker, not by a general rule.

Buying checklist for a 2013 CBR600RR

  1. Read the size, load index and speed symbol from your owner's manual or the tire label on the bike.
  2. Confirm your model year and market, because the official data above is for the 2013 European model.
  3. Check the manufacture date on any tire you buy (the last four digits of the DOT code show week and year) and follow the tire maker's guidance on age.
  4. Replace the tire when the tread reaches the wear indicator bars, or earlier if it is damaged.

FAQ

What tire size does a 2013 Honda CBR600RR take?

Honda's 2013 CBR600RR press pack lists 120/70-ZR17 at the front and 180/55-ZR17 at the rear.

What rim width does the 2013 CBR600RR have?

Honda lists 17M/C x MT3.5 at the front and 17M/C x MT5.5 at the rear.

When did the CBR600RR get ABS?

Honda says its electronic Combined ABS became available on the CBR600RR in 2009.

Can I use different tire sizes?

Only use a size approved by the motorcycle manufacturer or tire maker. Dunlop says a change should only be made "with the approval of the motorcycle or tire manufacturer."

What tire pressure should I run?

Use the figures on the tire information label on your bike or in your owner's manual. Honda's press pack does not list pressures.
