Suzuki GSX-R750 Tire Sizes: What Suzuki Publishes for 2006–2011 and 2021–2024

Published on June 14, 2026

Every Suzuki specification we could find for the GSX-R750 from 2006 onwards lists the same tire sizes: 120/70ZR17M/C (58W) at the front and 180/55ZR17M/C (73W) at the rear. That covers the 2006, 2008 and 2011 model changes in Suzuki's own archive and the 2021 to 2024 product pages. For the years in between, Suzuki doesn't publish a separate spec page we could read, so check the owner's manual or the tires on your bike.

We only list a figure when it appears in an official Suzuki document. Anything we couldn't source is left out.

How to read these numbers

  • 120/70ZR17M/C (58W): 120 mm nominal section width, sidewall height 70% of the width, 17-inch rim, M/C for motorcycle. 58 is the load index and W the speed symbol.
  • 180/55ZR17M/C (73W): 180 mm nominal width, sidewall 55% of the width, 17-inch rim, load index 73, speed symbol W.

Suzuki lists load index 58 front and 73 rear, with speed symbol W. Check your owner's manual before choosing a replacement.

What changed between the models?

The tire sizes stayed the same. These are the items in Suzuki's tables that did change:

Notes from Suzuki's text:

  • 2006: Suzuki describes a newly designed engine that is "34 mm lower and 57 mm shorter than the previous model."
  • 2008: based on the newly developed GSX-R600, with features including "electronically controlled steering dampers, a back-torque-limiting clutch, and the Suzuki Drive Mode" selector.
  • 2011: a new engine in the GSX-R600 chassis; Suzuki says curb mass is "8 kg lighter than the 2010 model with a 15 mm shorter wheelbase."
  • 2021–2024: the product pages list Brembo front brake calipers and the Suzuki Drive Mode Selector (SDMS).

Dry and wet weights aren't comparable with each other, so don't read the 2008-to-2011 change as a weight gain.

Which years and markets does this cover?

  • Documented: 2006, 2008 and 2011 (model-change tables in Suzuki's archive) and 2021–2024 (Global Suzuki product pages).
  • Not separately documented: 2007, 2009–2010 and 2012–2020. Suzuki's archive lists the model changes, and the tire sizes are the same before and after each one, but we didn't find a spec page for each of those years.
  • Markets: Global Suzuki pages describe the global model. Specifications can differ by market, so use your owner's manual or the tire label.

Rim width and tire pressure

Suzuki's archive and product pages don't list rim widths or tire pressures, and we didn't find an official GSX-R750 owner's manual we could open online. Use the pressures in your owner's manual or on the label on the bike. Don't use a figure from a forum, a different model year, or another model.

Can I fit a different size?

Only use a size approved by the motorcycle manufacturer or tire maker. Dunlop says a change should only be made "with the approval of the motorcycle or tire manufacturer."

That includes common questions such as fitting a 190-section rear. Every Suzuki table we found lists 180/55ZR17.

Buying checklist for a GSX-R750

  1. Confirm your model year and market.
  2. Compare the sizes in this table with your owner's manual or the tires fitted now.
  3. Note the load index (58 front, 73 rear) and speed symbol (W) Suzuki lists, and check them against your owner's manual.
  4. Take tire pressures from your owner's manual or the label on the bike.
  5. Follow your owner's manual on when to replace a tire.

FAQ

What tire size does a Suzuki GSX-R750 take?

Suzuki's specs list 120/70ZR17M/C (58W) front and 180/55ZR17M/C (73W) rear for the 2006, 2008 and 2011 models and for 2021 to 2024.

Did the GSX-R750 tire size change in 2011?

No. Suzuki's 2011 table lists the same front and rear sizes as 2006 and 2008.

Is the GSX-R750 rear tire 180 or 190?

Every Suzuki table we found lists 180/55ZR17. Only change size with the motorcycle or tire maker's approval.

What tire pressure should I run on a GSX-R750?

Use the figures in your owner's manual or on the label on the bike. Suzuki's spec pages don't list pressures.
