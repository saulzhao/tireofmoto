Kawasaki Z400 / ER-6n Tire Sizes: Front & Rear Guide (2013-2025)

March 17, 2026·Read article
❉ ShareFacebookCopy Link

Introduction

If you're looking for the correct tire sizes for your Kawasaki Z400 / ER-6n (2013-2025), this guide covers everything you need. Whether performing a scheduled replacement, upgrading grip levels, or just verifying OEM specifications, getting the right tire is critical for safety and handling.

OEM Tire Sizes (Factory Specifications)

The manufacturer specifies these exact dimensions for your Kawasaki Z400 / ER-6n:

  • Front tire size: 110/70-17
  • Rear tire size: 150/60-17

These sizes are optimized for the bike's weight distribution, wheelbase, and suspension geometry. Deviating from them can alter steering characteristics and may void your insurance.

Replacement Tire Guide

  1. Verify current tire size: Check the sidewall of your existing tire before purchasing a replacement.
  2. Load index and speed rating: Must meet or exceed OEM specifications for your model year.
  3. Tire type selection: Sport tires offer maximum grip but wear faster; touring compounds last longer with good wet weather performance.
  4. DOT date: Avoid tires manufactured more than 3 years before purchase (check the last 4 digits on sidewall).

Recommended Tire Brands for Kawasaki Z400 / ER-6n

The following brands produce OEM-quality replacement tires that fit your motorcycle:

  • Premium: Michelin Pilot Road, Bridgestone Battlax, Pirelli Diablo Rosso, Dunlop Q3+, Metzeler Roadtec
  • Mid-range: Toyo T1-R, IRC GP-1, Wulfsport Superbike, Tigar Sport Maxx
  • Budget: Avon Storm, Shinko E758, Mitas F940, Kumho Road King (availability varies by region)

Installation Tips

Always have your motorcycle tires mounted and balanced by a professional technician. The wheel should be dynamically balanced before installation, then verified with a static balancer after mounting. Recheck tire pressure and valve integrity after the first 50 miles of riding.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use a different tire size on my Kawasaki Z400 / ER-6n?

Minor deviations (±10%) are sometimes possible but will affect speedometer reading, steering geometry, and ground clearance. Consult your local tire specialist before making changes.

How often should tires be replaced?

Every 2-4 years by age or when tread depth reaches the wear indicators, whichever comes first. Sport bikes like the Kawasaki Z400 / ER-6n typically need rear tires replaced every 6,000-10,000 miles depending on riding style.
