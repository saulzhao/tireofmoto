Replacement Tips

  1. Match OEM size: Always replace with the same size or a manufacturer-approved alternative.
  2. Check DOT date: Avoid tires manufactured more than 3 years prior. The last 4 digits on sidewall show week/year.
  3. Load index: Must meet or exceed the OEM specification for your model year's weight rating.
  4. Spend accordingly: Tires are the single most important safety component. Don't cheap out.

Recommended Brands

  • Premium tire options: Michelin, Bridgestone, Pirelli, Dunlop, Metzeler
  • Mid-range alternatives: Toyo, IRC, Wulfsport, Shinko
  • Budget picks: Avon, Mitas, Kumho, Tigar (where available)

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I put a different tire size on my CFMOTO 450MT / NK 450?

Minor deviations are possible but can affect ABS calibration and speedometer accuracy. Consult the tire manufacturer's fitment guide before making changes.

How long do motorcycle tires last?

Sport bikes typically need rear tire replacement every 5,000-10,000 miles depending on riding style. Touring tires can last 20,000+ miles. Always replace by age (5 years max) regardless of tread depth.
