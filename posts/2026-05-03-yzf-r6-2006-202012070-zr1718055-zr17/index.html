Yamaha YZF-R6 2008–2020 Tire Sizes: What Yamaha Publishes

Published on May 3, 2026

The YZF-R6 kept the same tire sizes for a long time, but "the R6 takes 120/70 and 180/55" is only half the answer. The load index and speed symbol matter when you buy, and the bike changed a lot in 2017. This page sets out what Yamaha's own European spec sheets say for each model year from 2008 to 2020, and what you still need to check on your own bike.

We only list a figure when it appears in an official Yamaha document. Anything we could not source is left out rather than estimated.

Tire sizes by model year

Yamaha Motor Europe publishes a spec sheet ("factsheet") for each model year. These are the tire entries for the YZF-R6:

Model yearsFront tireRear tireMarked tubeless on the sheet
2008–2016120/70 ZR17M/C (58W)180/55 ZR17M/C (73W)Not stated
2017–2020120/70 ZR17M/C (58W) Tubeless180/55 ZR17M/C (73W) TubelessYes

Source: Yamaha Motor Europe, YZF-R6 spec sheets (UK English) for 2008, 2009, 2010, 2011, 2012, 2013, 2014, 2015, 2016, 2017, 2018, 2019 and 2020. Every one of the 13 sheets gives the same front and rear sizes.

The sheets for 2008 to 2016 don't include the word "Tubeless" in the tire line; the 2017 to 2020 sheets do. That's a change in how the sheet is written. It is not, on its own, evidence that the earlier bikes used tubes, so don't read it that way.

How to read these numbers

  • 120/70 ZR17M/C (58W): 120 mm nominal section width, sidewall height 70% of the width, ZR speed-category marking, 17-inch rim, M/C for motorcycle. 58 is the load index and W the speed symbol.
  • 180/55 ZR17M/C (73W): 180 mm nominal width, sidewall 55% of the width, 17-inch rim; load index 73, speed symbol W.

When you buy, match the load index and speed symbol Yamaha lists, and don't go below them.

What changed in 2017

Yamaha's 2017 sheet describes "the new YZF-R6" with new styling, "YZF-R1 type front forks", "dual front discs", a 6-mode traction control system, a quick shifter "and ABS". Comparing the 2016 and 2017 sheets:

Front suspensionUpside-down telescopic fork, Ø41 mmTelescopic forks, Ø43 mm

So the 2017 update changed the forks and brakes, but Yamaha kept the same tire sizes, load indexes and speed symbols.

ABS

The 2017, 2018, 2019 and 2020 sheets list ABS among the features ("YCC-T, YCC-I, ABS and D-Mode"). The 2008–2016 sheets we read don't mention ABS. We aren't going to say more than that about earlier bikes without a Yamaha document that covers it.

Which years and markets does this cover?

  • 2008–2020: Yamaha Motor Europe spec sheets, UK English. These describe the European model.
  • 2006 and 2007: the Yamaha Motor Europe sheets for these years returned an access error when we tried them, and we didn't find another Yamaha document we could read. That's why this page starts at 2008.
  • Other markets (for example the US, Japan or Australia): we did not use their documents here. Specifications can differ by market.

If your bike is from another market or year, read the tire information in your owner's manual or on the bike. That is written for your exact model.

Rim width and tire pressure

Yamaha's spec sheets don't list rim widths or tire pressures, and we didn't find an official YZF-R6 owner's manual we could read online. Use the pressures in your owner's manual or on the label on the bike. Don't use a figure from a forum or another model.

Can I fit a different size?

Only use a size approved by the motorcycle manufacturer or tire maker. Dunlop says a change should only be made "with the approval of the motorcycle or tire manufacturer."

That applies to common questions such as fitting a 190-section rear or a different profile. Yamaha's sheets list 180/55 ZR17 for every year from 2008 to 2020.

Buying checklist for a YZF-R6

  1. Confirm your model year and market.
  2. For a European 2008–2020 bike, the sizes are 120/70 ZR17 (58W) front and 180/55 ZR17 (73W) rear. For any other bike, read your owner's manual or the tire label.
  3. Match the load index and speed symbol, and never go below them.
  4. Check the manufacture date on any tire you buy (the last four digits of the DOT code show week and year) and follow the tire maker's guidance on age.
  5. Replace the tire when the tread reaches the wear indicator bars, or earlier if it is damaged.

FAQ

What tire size does a Yamaha YZF-R6 take?

Yamaha Motor Europe's spec sheets for every model year from 2008 to 2020 list 120/70 ZR17M/C (58W) front and 180/55 ZR17M/C (73W) rear.

Did the 2017 YZF-R6 change tire size?

No. Yamaha's 2017 sheet lists the same sizes, load indexes and speed symbols as 2016. The 2017 changes Yamaha lists include 43 mm forks, 320 mm front discs and ABS.

What load index does the YZF-R6 need?

Yamaha lists 58 at the front and 73 at the rear, both with speed symbol W.

Can I use different tire sizes?

Only use a size approved by the motorcycle manufacturer or tire maker. Dunlop says a change should only be made "with the approval of the motorcycle or tire manufacturer."

What tire pressure should I run?

Use the figures in your owner's manual or on the label on the bike. Yamaha's spec sheets don't list pressures.
