Kawasaki Ninja ZX-10R 2019–2024 Tire Sizes: What Kawasaki Publishes

Published on May 20, 2026

Every Ninja ZX-10R and ZX-10RR spec sheet that Kawasaki USA publishes for 2019 to 2024 lists the same tire sizes: 120/70 ZR17 at the front and 190/55 ZR17 at the rear. The sheets don't give a load index or a speed symbol. Read those from the tires on your bike or from your owner's manual. The tires stayed the same, but the chassis did not: the 2021 sheets show a longer wheelbase, new aerodynamic parts and a TFT display.

We only list a figure when it appears in an official Kawasaki document. Anything we couldn't source is left out.

How to read these numbers

  • 120/70 ZR17: 120 mm nominal section width, sidewall height 70% of the width, 17-inch rim.
  • 190/55 ZR17: 190 mm nominal width, sidewall 55% of the width, 17-inch rim.

That's all the sheets give. A replacement tire also carries a load index and a speed symbol, which the sheets don't list. Take them from the tires fitted to your bike or from your owner's manual, and don't go below them.

What about 2016 to 2018?

The current page title covers 2016 onwards. We couldn't read Kawasaki's own figures for those years. The 2017 and 2018 spec sheets download from Kawasaki's site, but their text can't be extracted. We found no 2016 sheet at the same address pattern. So this page doesn't state sizes for 2016–2018. For a bike from those years, use the owner's manual or the size printed on the tires fitted at the factory.

A few notes from the sheets:

  • 2019: the ABS sheet gives curb weights of 454.2 lb (ABS) and 449.8 lb (non-ABS). Rider aids listed: launch control, KIBS, S-KTRC traction control, engine brake control, a quick shifter ("dual direction") and Kawasaki Cornering Management Function (KCMF).
  • 2021: the sheet adds Electronic Cruise Control and lists "Aerodynamic Downforce Generating Devices" and TFT instrumentation. Wheelbase goes from 56.7 in to 57.1 in, and curb weight is listed as 456.4 lb.
  • 2023: the sheet at the ABS address doesn't list KIBS and gives a curb weight of 452.0 lb. That matches the non-ABS figure on the 2024 sheet. The sheet doesn't explain why.
  • 2024: the sheet lists an Öhlins Electronic Steering Damper, a "4.3" Full-Color TFT" display and smartphone connectivity. It gives 456.4 lb (ABS) and 452.0 lb (non-ABS).

ZX-10RR and KRT Edition

  • ZX-10RR: the 2019, 2021 and 2023 RR sheets list the same 120/70 ZR17 front and 190/55 ZR17 rear as the ZX-10R. The 2021 RR sheet lists "Pankl Pistons & Titanium Connecting Rods" under special features.
  • KRT Edition: the 2024 KRT Edition sheet lists the same tire sizes as the 2024 ZX-10R.
  • Other versions: we didn't find an SE spec sheet on Kawasaki's US site, so this page doesn't cover it.

Which markets does this cover?

The sheets come from Kawasaki USA and describe US models. Specifications can differ by market. If your bike was sold elsewhere, use your owner's manual or the tire label.

Rim width and tire pressure

Kawasaki's spec sheets don't list rim widths or tire pressures. The owner's manual is the place to look, and Kawasaki's Owners Center asks for the bike's details before it shows the manual. Use the pressures in your owner's manual or on the label on the bike. Don't use a figure from a forum, a different model year or another model.

Can I fit a different size?

Only use a size approved by the motorcycle manufacturer or tire maker. Dunlop says a change should only be made "with the approval of the motorcycle or tire manufacturer."

That applies to common questions such as fitting a 200-section rear. Kawasaki's sheets list 190/55 ZR17 for every ZX-10R and ZX-10RR sheet we read from 2019 to 2024.

Buying checklist for a ZX-10R

  1. Confirm your model year, version (ZX-10R, ZX-10RR or KRT Edition), market and whether your bike has ABS.
  2. For a US 2019–2024 bike, the sizes are 120/70 ZR17 front and 190/55 ZR17 rear. For any other bike, read your owner's manual or the tire label.
  3. Take the load index and speed symbol from your owner's manual or the current tires, and never go below them.
  4. Follow the tire maker's guidance on tire age.
  5. Follow your owner's manual and the tire maker's guidance on when to replace a tire.

FAQ

What tire size does a 2019–2024 Kawasaki ZX-10R take?

Kawasaki's US spec sheets list 120/70 ZR17 front and 190/55 ZR17 rear for every year from 2019 to 2024.

Does the ZX-10RR use different tire sizes?

Not on the sheets we read. The 2019, 2021 and 2023 ZX-10RR sheets list the same 120/70 ZR17 and 190/55 ZR17.

Did the 2021 ZX-10R change tire size?

No. The 2021 sheet keeps the same sizes. It lists a longer wheelbase (57.1 in), aerodynamic downforce devices, cruise control and a TFT display.

What load index, speed rating and tire pressure does the ZX-10R need?

Kawasaki's spec sheets don't list them. Use the figures in your owner's manual, on the label on the bike, or on the tires fitted to your bike.
