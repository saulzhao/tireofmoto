Kawasaki Ninja ZX-6R 2019–2024 Tire Sizes: What Kawasaki Publishes

Published on May 19, 2026

Kawasaki's US spec sheets list the same tire sizes for every Ninja ZX-6R from 2019 to 2024: 120/70 ZR17 at the front and 180/55 ZR17 at the rear. The sheets don't give a load index or speed symbol, so read those from the tires on your bike or your owner's manual. Other parts of the bike did change over those years, most visibly in 2024.

We only list a figure when it appears in an official Kawasaki document. Anything we couldn't source is left out.

How to read these numbers

  • 120/70 ZR17: 120 mm nominal section width, sidewall height 70% of the width, ZR speed-category marking, 17-inch rim.
  • 180/55 ZR17: 180 mm nominal width, sidewall 55% of the width, 17-inch rim.

The sheets stop there. A replacement tire also carries a load index and a speed symbol, and Kawasaki's spec sheets don't list them. Take them from the tires fitted to your bike or from your owner's manual, and don't go below them.

A few notes from the sheets:

  • 2019: the 2019 ABS sheet lists a rear disc of 210 mm. The 2020 and later sheets list 220 mm. The sheets don't explain the difference.
  • 2019–2023: the rider aids listed are Kawasaki Traction Control (KTRC), Power Mode and Kawasaki Quick Shifter (KQS, upshift only), plus KIBS on the 2019, 2020 and 2023 sheets. The 2021 and 2022 sheets don't list KIBS.
  • 2024: the sheet lists "Power Modes (Full/Low)", semi-floating front discs, a "4.3" Full-Color TFT Instrumentation" and smartphone connectivity. It gives curb weights for ABS and non-ABS bikes in both 50-state and 49-state versions: 436.6 lb and 432.2 lb (50-state), 434.4 lb and 430.0 lb (49-state).

Which years and markets does this cover?

  • 2019–2024, US: Kawasaki USA spec sheets. These describe the US models.
  • ABS and non-ABS: for 2019 and 2020 we read the ABS sheets; the 2019 sheet also gives a non-ABS curb weight. We couldn't read the 2019 non-ABS sheet, and the 2020 non-ABS sheet wasn't available.
  • Special editions such as KRT and anniversary models have their own sheets, which we didn't compare here.
  • Other markets: specifications can differ by market. Use your owner's manual or the tire label.

Rim width and tire pressure

Kawasaki's spec sheets don't list rim widths or tire pressures, and we didn't find an official ZX-6R owner's manual we could open online. Use the pressures in your owner's manual or on the label on the bike. Don't use a figure from a forum, a different model year, or another model.

Can I fit a different size?

Only use a size approved by the motorcycle manufacturer or tire maker. Dunlop says a change should only be made "with the approval of the motorcycle or tire manufacturer."

That applies to common questions such as fitting a 190-section rear. Kawasaki's sheets list 180/55 ZR17 for every year from 2019 to 2024.

Buying checklist for a ZX-6R

  1. Confirm your model year, market and whether your bike has ABS.
  2. For a US 2019–2024 bike, the sizes are 120/70 ZR17 front and 180/55 ZR17 rear. For any other bike, read your owner's manual or the tire label.
  3. Take the load index and speed symbol from your owner's manual or the current tires, and never go below them.
  4. Check the manufacture date on any tire you buy and follow the tire maker's guidance on age.
  5. Follow your owner's manual on when to replace a tire.

FAQ

What tire size does a 2019–2024 Kawasaki ZX-6R take?

Kawasaki's US spec sheets list 120/70 ZR17 front and 180/55 ZR17 rear for every year from 2019 to 2024.

Did the 2024 ZX-6R change tire size?

No. The 2024 sheet lists the same sizes as 2019. It lists semi-floating front discs, "Power Modes (Full/Low)" and a "4.3" Full-Color TFT Instrumentation".

What load index and speed rating does the ZX-6R need?

Kawasaki's spec sheets don't list them. Use the figures in your owner's manual or on the tires fitted to your bike.

What tire pressure should I run?

Use the figures in your owner's manual or on the label on the bike. Kawasaki's spec sheets don't list pressures.
