Honda CB650R Tire Sizes: 2019, 2022 and Current UK Model

April 11, 2026 · By Zenyare Tire
𝕏 Share Facebook Copy Link

Honda lists a 120/70 front and 180/55 rear on 17-inch wheels for the CB650R (120/70ZR17 and 180/55ZR17 in the 2019 and current UK specs). The sizes are the same in the three official Honda specs we checked: Honda Europe's 2019 launch specification, American Honda's 2022 specification and Honda UK's current specification for the CB650R E-Clutch.

We didn't find official Honda specs for the other model years, so if your CB650R is from a year not listed here, check your owner's manual or the tires on the bike.

Each column is copied as Honda printed it. American Honda's 2022 table writes the sizes in short form, without "ZR" or the load and speed marking. Honda UK's current page prints "(58W)" for the front tire and no marking for the rear. Honda Europe's 2019 specification says "All specifications are provisional and subject to change without notice."

Honda's own wheel descriptions also differ: "6-spoke" in the 2019 specification and "5-Spoke" on the current UK page; American Honda's 2022 release describes "five Y-shaped spokes." Only the current UK page gives rim widths.

How to read the sizes

  • 120/70ZR17: 120 mm nominal width, sidewall height 70% of the width, 17-inch rim.
  • 180/55ZR17: 180 mm nominal width, sidewall height 55% of the width, 17-inch rim.
  • (58W) and (73W): as printed by Honda. When you replace tires, follow your owner's manual for the markings to match.

What about tire pressure?

None of the three Honda specifications lists tire pressures. Use the figures in your owner's manual or on the tire information label on your bike.

Did the tire size change between years?

Not in the three specs we checked. The 2019, 2022 and current specifications all list 120/70 front and 180/55 rear on 17-inch wheels. For years we didn't check, confirm with your manual.

Is the CB650R E-Clutch different?

Honda UK's current page is for the CB650R E-Clutch, and it lists the same 120/70 front and 180/55 rear 17-inch sizes as the 2019 and 2022 specifications.

Can I fit a different size?

Only use a size approved by the motorcycle manufacturer or tire maker. Dunlop says a change should only be made "with the approval of the motorcycle or tire manufacturer."

Buying checklist for a CB650R

  1. Check the size on your current tires' sidewalls: 120/70 front and 180/55 rear on 17-inch wheels in Honda's specs (120/70ZR17 and 180/55ZR17 in the 2019 and UK specs).
  2. If your bike is from a year not listed above, confirm the sizes in your owner's manual.
  3. Match the markings your owner's manual specifies.
  4. Set cold pressures from your manual or the label on the bike.

FAQ

What tire size does the Honda CB650R take?

Honda lists 120/70 front and 180/55 rear on 17-inch wheels in its 2019, 2022 and current UK specifications (2019 and UK: 120/70ZR17 and 180/55ZR17).

What rim sizes does the CB650R have?

Honda UK lists 17M/C X MT3.50 front and 17M/C X MT5.50 rear for the current CB650R E-Clutch. The 2019 and 2022 specs we checked don't give rim widths.

Does the CB650R E-Clutch use different tires?

No. Honda UK lists 120/70ZR17 front and 180/55ZR17 rear for it, the same 120/70 front and 180/55 rear 17-inch sizes as the 2019 and 2022 specifications.
